**To the Moon** (hereinafter "this blog", `https://junstellar.github.io`) values your privacy and explains transparently what information is collected and how it is used.

## 1. Personal Information We Collect

This blog is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages and has **no sign-up, login, or comment features.** Therefore, it does **not directly collect any personally identifiable information** such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

If you contact us directly via the email address listed under "Contact" below, the information contained in your email (your email address and the content of your inquiry) is used solely to respond to you and is not stored or shared separately.

## 2. Visitor Analytics (GoatCounter)

This blog uses **GoatCounter**, a privacy-first analytics tool, to understand visitor traffic. GoatCounter:

- **Does not use cookies** and does not identify individuals.
- Collects only **anonymous, aggregated statistics** such as pages visited, country, browser, and referrer.
- Uses IP addresses only temporarily (e.g., to prevent duplicate counting) and does not store them in an identifiable form.

For details, see the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## 3. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

This blog may display ads through **Google AdSense**. Please note the following:

- **Third-party vendors, including Google,** use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this blog and other websites.
- Google uses advertising cookies (e.g., the **DoubleClick cookie**) to serve ads based on your interests.
- You can **opt out of personalized advertising** in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
- To opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies, visit [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

For more about how Google uses data in advertising, see [Google's advertising privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 4. How to Refuse Cookies

You can refuse cookies through your web browser settings. Note that if you block cookies, some ads may be shown as generic rather than personalized ads.

- **Chrome**: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Edge**: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
- **Safari**: Preferences → Privacy

## 5. Children Under 14

This blog is not directed at children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
